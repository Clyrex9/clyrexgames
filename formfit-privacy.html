FormFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

FormFit — Photo & Signature Resizer is an app by Clyrex. This policy explains how the current version handles your images and data.

Photos and processing

You choose individual photos or signature images using the device's picker. FormFit processes them on your device. The app does not upload images to a server, and Clyrex does not receive your images. When you tap Save, the output is copied to your device's photo library. When you tap Share, your device's share sheet sends the output to the destination you select; that destination's privacy practices then apply.

Metadata

FormFit creates a newly encoded output image. The original file's EXIF metadata, including GPS location metadata, is not copied into the exported image.

Local history and retention

Recent export details and output files are stored locally on your device so you can reopen results in History. Clyrex cannot access this history. You can remove the app's local data through your device's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Images you have saved to your photo library or shared elsewhere are separate copies and must be deleted from those locations separately.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

FormFit does not create accounts and currently has no advertising or analytics SDK. The app does not collect or share personal data with Clyrex or third parties. No backend account or cloud storage is used.

Permissions

FormFit uses the system image picker to access only images you choose. On older Android versions, saving an output to the photo library may require a storage write permission. FormFit does not request broad, ongoing photo-library read access.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy before changing the app's data practices and will revise the date above. For privacy questions, contact clyrex9@gmail.com.
