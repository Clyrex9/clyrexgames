FROM 3.9 TO 4.0

Making the whole career feel connected.

A football career is more than the final score. It is the card you risk, the team you grow with, the people you meet and the stories that follow you off the pitch. For 4.0, the goal was to bring all of that closer together.

Your feedback shaped this update: confusing actions, repeated conversations, inconsistent artwork and weeks that could get stuck. Alongside the visual overhaul, the everyday parts of a career needed attention.

01 / YOUR CAREER

A career hub with a clear next move.

The career centre has a shared visual theme across its tabs. Your next fixture, energy, form and coach trust are easier to find. Team and league crests follow the same identity, so moving between screens feels like staying in one football world.

Achievements and season goals have a stronger presentation, while career stories give each season more context. The match, training and social parts of your week sit closer together.

02 / MATCHES & CARDS

Play the card.
See the move.

Card artwork was reviewed against the movement it is meant to show: rabona crosses, nutmegs, one-twos and goalkeeper actions. A card should tell you what you are about to attempt before you even read its name.

The match commentary screen now carries the pitch action, with replays tied to what happened. Decision moments keep the footballer artwork and make room for your cards on smaller screens. The aim is to make each choice easier to read and its outcome easier to follow.

03 / THE FOOTBALL WORLD

Fresh lineups.
A shared identity.

Starting club lists were checked and updated across 15 countries. New careers use 734 clubs across 37 league divisions. Team colours, country badges and league identities were brought into a more consistent visual system.

Existing careers keep their own saved football world. The update refreshes starting memberships; it does not claim to reproduce every real-world playoff or tournament format.

04 / OFF THE PITCH

People, not just profile cards.

Character portraits now have more individual styles, and relationship stories, dialogue and consequences received another pass. Team relationships and career events also give life outside a match more room to develop.

DeckTalk brings club accounts, sports media and fans into the same feed. More distinct profiles and a better-balanced follower curve help your reputation grow with your career.

05 / THE EVERYDAY DETAILS

An easier first week.
A smoother next season.

The quieter changes matter every time you open the game:

  • A guided visual tutorial shows you around the career screens. Returning players can also see the new tour once and choose to skip it.
  • Weekly progression fixes cover low-energy and injury situations, including weeks where resting has already been used.
  • Swipe fixes restore the connection between liking a character and starting a conversation.
  • Deck suggestions and card purchase feedback make it harder to forget a new card in your collection.
  • Audio handling and unused assets were cleaned up to reduce unnecessary work and keep the package lighter.
ONE DEVELOPER. A SHARED JOURNEY.

Thank you for sticking with DeckBall.

14,500 downloads and 117 Google Play reviews are a lot of people for a solo project. Every thoughtful review, bug report and screenshot helped decide what 4.0 should become. This update is a step toward making the game feel as complete as the careers you build in it.

4.0 is being prepared for release. These images are from the development build; the current Google Play version remains available while the update is readied.
