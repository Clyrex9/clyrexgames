FROM 3.9 TO 4.0

Making the whole career feel connected.

In 3.9, we worked on goalkeeper careers, performance-based transfers and relationships. 4.0 keeps those systems and adds more match situations, ongoing personal stories, season reviews and new tools for your career.

Below, the additions come first, followed by the screens and existing content that were reworked. Goalkeeper mode, DeckTalk, marriage and children were already in the game; 4.0 connects them more closely to the rest of your career.

ADDED IN 4.0

Behind the new look,
there is more to play.

Decisions that carry into the next week, teammates with their own concerns, and a season that leaves a story behind.

01

From 9 generic situations to 24 match moments

One-twos, cutbacks, escaping a press, interceptions, last-man cover and goalkeeper interventions are selected for your position. The same moment does not appear twice in one match. Teammate support and the opponent’s playing style affect your chances; turning a good pass into a goal also depends on the teammate finishing it.

02

A Career Journal and five story paths

The coach, selection competition, derby, media and transfer paths each have three chapters. Saying “I will work on it” is not enough: later chapters check your actual training, rest and match appearances. The journal keeps your promises and the outcomes of your last 12 stories.

03

Beyond the dressing room

The captain, young player, veteran and selection rival have personal concerns of their own. Four paths with 12 chapters: supporting a young teammate struggling with comments, or a captain carrying everyone’s problems. Bonds develop through real conversations and activities across different weeks.

04

Two new people: Elif and Derya

The partner roster grows from six to eight. Sound designer Elif’s studio project and Derya’s neighbourhood and city work shape their conversations. The two additions bring 20 main conversations. All eight partners now have 24 personal story chapters in total, with individual expectations and preferences.

05

Relationships remember the next season

Finding time together and making room for personal goals open new topics in later seasons. Broken promises or unwanted public posts can lead to a trust process. Gifts alone cannot build the relationship: attention across different weeks, attending dates and respecting boundaries matter.

06

The echo of a public post

A date photo, transfer rumour or controversial statement can start one of three four-chapter public stories. Your first reply is remembered in later weeks. A promise to answer on the pitch requires actual play and training; a rumour cannot create a transfer offer by itself.

07

Season reviews and an awards night

After your transfer decision, a three-stage ceremony presents your team result, trophies you actually won and personal performance. A season archive and trophy cabinet preserve that history. Outfield players and keepers are evaluated using their own statistics; the Golden Ball follows DeckBall’s in-game award conditions.

08

A clearer first week and an easier deck setup

The old daily “play a match / train once” guidance is replaced by an eight-step visual tutorial. Skip it or replay it in settings. Purchase receipts, deck suggestions and an eight-slot active deck layout help you bring a newly acquired card into your matches.

REWORKED FOR 4.0

Familiar systems, with a stronger presentation.

The career hub, main menu, match screens, card art, equipment, country and league crests now share a visual direction. All 159 existing major events were rewritten across eight languages; these are not 159 newly added events. Micro-event writing and 12 scene illustrations were refreshed too.

01 / YOUR CAREER

A career hub with a clear next move.

The career centre has a shared visual theme across its tabs. Your next fixture, energy, form and coach trust are easier to find. Team and league crests follow the same identity, so moving between screens feels like staying in one football world.

The achievement showcase presents the existing 28 badges with distinct frames, progress targets and earned notifications. New season goals and the Career Journal give you direction beyond winning the next match. Tapping an open bottom-bar tab again closes it.

02 / MATCHES & CARDS

Play the card.
See the move.

Card artwork was reviewed against the movement it is meant to show: rabona crosses, nutmegs, one-twos and goalkeeper actions. A card should tell you what you are about to attempt before you even read its name.

After you choose a card, its outcome plays on the live pitch in the commentary screen and the match continues. The action follows the real card result and score. Decision moments retain footballer artwork and keep three options accessible on smaller screens. A pass that creates a team goal earns an assist; a dribble does not automatically count as one.

03 / THE FOOTBALL WORLD

Fresh lineups.
A shared identity.

Starting club lists were checked and updated across 15 countries. New careers use 734 clubs across 37 league divisions. Team colours, country badges and league identities were brought into a more consistent visual system.

Starting memberships and European competition entries were updated; team colours and crests in the league screen share one source. The 734 clubs are the updated total, not the number of additions. Existing careers keep their own saved football world.

04 / OFF THE PITCH

People, not just profile cards.

The six existing characters received more individual portraits and refreshed conversations. Date preferences, trust and time actually spent together shape relationship progress. Repeated gifts have less impact; high closeness alone does not guarantee exclusivity or an accepted proposal.

DeckTalk now includes club accounts using the in-game club’s crest, distinct media identities and varied fan portraits. Post variation reduces repetition. Follower growth is balanced around league visibility, minutes played and performance, rather than multiplying every week in lower leagues.

05 / THE EVERYDAY DETAILS

An easier first week.
A smoother next season.

The quieter changes matter every time you open the game:

  • A guided visual tutorial shows you around the career screens. Returning players can also see the new tour once and choose to skip it.
  • Weekly progression fixes cover low-energy and injury situations, including weeks where resting has already been used.
  • Swipe fixes restore the connection between liking a character and starting a conversation.
  • Deck suggestions and card purchase feedback make it harder to forget a new card in your collection.
  • Audio handling and unused assets were cleaned up to reduce unnecessary work and keep the package lighter.
ONE DEVELOPER. A SHARED JOURNEY.

Thank you for sticking with DeckBall.

14,500 downloads and 117 Google Play reviews are a lot of people for a solo project. Every thoughtful review, bug report and screenshot helped decide what 4.0 should become. This update is a step toward making the game feel as complete as the careers you build in it.

4.0 is being prepared for release. These images are from the development build; the current Google Play version remains available while the update is readied.
